KINX KK cross-reference: HIWIN KK50 / KK60 / KK86 part numbers

56 part numbers · CSV and JSON · CC BY 4.0 · published 13 November 2026 by KINX Linear Modules, Humen, Dongguan, China.

This table maps HIWIN-type KK linear module part numbers to the four things that decide whether a replacement fits: frame size, screw lead, rail length and maximum stroke with one block, plus the accuracy grade letter in the part number. The same data is maintained on the manufacturer's site at kinxfa.com/pages/hiwin-kk-part-number-decoder, and this repository is the machine-readable mirror.

Columns

Part number           HIWIN-type part number, e.g. KK6010P-400A1-F0
Size                  KK50 / KK60 / KK86
Lead                  screw lead in mm
Grade                 Normal (C) or Precision (P)
Rail                  rail length in mm
Max stroke (1 block)  maximum stroke with one block, where published
KINX build            the KINX model that matches the mounting dimensions
Quote                 how to price it

Read this before you order against the table. The part numbers and the Max stroke (1 block) column reproduce values published on the manufacturer's page. They are a starting point for a drawing check, not a guarantee of interchangeability: combined height, hole pitch and thread, motor flange and coupling must be confirmed on the supplier's drawing before a purchase order. Where the column says confirm on drawing, no published value exists.

Scope — what is not in this table

KK40, KK100 and KK130 are not built by KINX and are therefore absent. Long-stroke builds above 1310 mm usable stroke are quoted as custom bodies rather than as part-number matches. Belt-driven and aluminium-profile modules use a different numbering system and are not covered here.
